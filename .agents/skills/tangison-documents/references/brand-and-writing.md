## Adaptive client brand

1. Use supplied brand guides and original logo files first.
2. If the user supplies a website, verify the logo and dominant colours from the live site before building.
3. Separate primary, secondary, neutral, and tint colours. Keep long-form text neutral and dark.
4. Preserve the client's visual character. Do not force Tangison teal, Poppins, or Tangison motifs into client-branded work.
5. If the logo has several variants, choose the one with adequate contrast for each background. Never add an improvised box behind a logo unless it is part of the brand system.
6. Use monochrome logo variants only if officially supplied or if a neutral rendering can be created without altering the mark's structure.

## Human writing pass

Apply these passes in order:

### 1. Purpose

State why the document exists and what the reader must understand, decide, approve, pay, sign, or do next.

### 2. Evidence

Replace vague claims with supplied facts, deliverables, dates, quantities, constraints, proof, or examples. Delete claims that cannot be supported.

### 3. Voice

Use natural business English appropriate to Namibia and the actual relationship. Preserve forms of address such as “The Director,” “Mr.,” or a familiar name when supplied.

### 4. Compression

Remove empty phrases such as:

- “In today's fast-paced world”
- “We are delighted to present”
- “At the heart of our approach”
- “This comprehensive solution is designed to”
- “Leveraging cutting-edge technology”
- “We believe that this proposal represents”

Open with substance instead.

### 5. Rhythm

Vary sentence length. Do not make every section a heading followed by three generic sentences and three bullets. Use a short lead, a fuller explanation, and a precise list only where useful.

### 6. Accuracy

Check every name, date, reference number, URL, telephone number, bank digit, currency, arithmetic result, and defined term against the source material.

## Context notes and estimates

Use a note only when a figure could be misunderstood without context. Label the note accurately:

- `Estimate` for a reasoned approximation.
- `Assumption` for an input not yet confirmed.
- `Excludes` for out-of-scope cost or work.
- `Payment milestone` for amounts tied to delivery stages.
- `Validity` for a quotation expiry.

Do not call every note a “tip.” Do not place a coloured box beneath every number. Group related assumptions into one compact note where possible.

## Print economy

- Default to ink-light for letters, invoices, quotations, agreements, and manuals intended for office printing.
- Use white or bone pages with dark text and thin accent rules.
- Avoid full-page dark backgrounds, saturated table headers, and large solid logo fills.
- Test pale colours in grayscale. Important information must survive monochrome printing.
- Use presentation-rich treatment only for digital proposals, profiles, reports, and certificates when visual impact is part of the purpose.

## Logo asset preparation

- Prefer SVG or transparent PNG. Use high-resolution raster files only when no vector exists.
- Trim accidental transparent padding without cropping the actual mark.
- Preserve the original aspect ratio.
- Create derivatives only for production needs: high-resolution transparent PNG, official monochrome version, or low-opacity watermark image.
- Keep the original untouched.
- Never generate a replacement logo with an image model.
